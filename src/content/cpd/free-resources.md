Keeping your CPD current shouldn't mean spending a fortune or wading through
marketing fluff. Below is a **curated, annotated directory** of free (and a few
low-cost) CPD resources for property professionals — grouped by topic, each with
a note on what it covers, who it suits, whether it's accredited, and my honest
take. The curation is the point: everything here earns its place.

<blockquote class="warn">
<strong>Please verify before you rely on anything.</strong> Providers change
their offerings, and "free" sometimes becomes "registration required" or
"members only". I check entries periodically, but confirm the current position
on the provider's own site.

## Damp & mould

**RICS — damp & mould guidance and CPD**
What it covers: professional standards and guidance on investigating damp and
mould. · Who it suits: surveyors and housing professionals. · Free/paid: mixed
(some member-only). · Accredited: yes (RICS). · *Dom's take:* the professional
baseline — start here for the "what good looks like" view.

**Property Care Association (PCA) — webinars & resources**
What it covers: damp, condensation, timber and remediation from the trade body. ·
Who it suits: practitioners doing remedial work. · Free/paid: mixed. ·
Accredited: PCA. · *Dom's take:* strong on the practical remediation side;
read alongside independent guidance.

**Damp & mould podcasts**
What it covers: practitioner discussion of diagnosis, Awaab's Law and real cases.
· Who it suits: anyone wanting CPD on the commute. · Free/paid: free. ·
Accredited: no (informal — log and reflect). · *Dom's take:* great for staying
current; pair with primary sources before acting on anything.

## Energy & EPC

**Elmhurst Energy / accreditation-scheme webinars**
What it covers: EPC methodology, SAP/RdSAP updates, energy assessment practice. ·
Who it suits: DEAs and trainees. · Free/paid: many free webinars. · Accredited:
scheme CPD. · *Dom's take:* the most useful free way to keep on top of
methodology changes in my own field.

## Retrofit & PAS 2035

**The Retrofit Academy — free resources**
What it covers: retrofit fundamentals, PAS 2035 context, career routes. · Who it
suits: aspiring and practising retrofit assessors/coordinators. · Free/paid:
mixed (free intro content). · Accredited: yes (paid courses). · *Dom's take:*
good orientation before you commit to a paid qualification.

## Building pathology

**Building-pathology webinars & manufacturer CPD**

Free/paid: usually free. · Accredited: varies. · *Dom's take:* manufacturer CPD

## Regulatory (Awaab's Law, MEES, HHSRS)

**Awaab's Law — official guidance**
What it covers: the duties on landlords to act on damp and mould within set
timescales. · Who it suits: anyone working in housing condition. · Free/paid:
free. · Accredited: n/a (primary source). · *Dom's take:* the single most
important regulatory development in this space — know it cold.

Free/paid: guidance free; formal training paid. · Accredited: varies. · *Dom's
